# Images to generate

Each placeholder on the site names a file. Save the finished image as `public/images/<name>.webp`
(`.jpg` and `.png` also work) and it replaces the placeholder on the next build. No code change needed.

**Art direction for every image:** documentary photography, real places and real hands, evening or
low light, near-black shadows, one small red light from the device as the only strong colour.
No text, no logos, no screens with readable content, no faces looking at the camera, no sci-fi glow.
